I'm Ray, a genetics PhD student at the University of North Texas, and I'm moving into data engineering. My research uses the nematode _C. elegans_ to study gene family evolution and stress response, and much of that work has meant writing code to wrangle sequencing and microscopy data into something I could analyze. Building those pipelines turned out to be the part I enjoyed most, so that's where I'm heading next.

This blog is where I write about what I'm learning along the way, from data pipelines and tooling to the occasional bit of biology.
